# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

This policy describes how employees of Lumen Analytics Ltd (a fictional company) may work outside the office.

## Hybrid working pattern

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Teams agree one shared "anchor day" each week when
everyone is expected in the office. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from a department head.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone. Outside core hours employees may arrange their
working time flexibly, as long as they work their contracted hours.

## Home office equipment

Each employee can claim a one-time home office allowance of £300 for a desk, chair or monitor. Claims are made
through the expenses process with receipts. Company laptops and phones are provided by IT and remain company
property.

## Working abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with approval from their
line manager and HR at least 4 weeks in advance. Some countries are excluded for tax or security reasons; HR
keeps the current list.

## Security when working remotely

Only use trusted networks. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN switched on. Screens must be locked
whenever the laptop is left unattended, and confidential calls should not be taken in public places.
