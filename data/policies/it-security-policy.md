# IT Security Policy

Rules every employee of Lumen Analytics Ltd (a fictional company) must follow to keep company data safe.

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and unique to each system. Use the company password manager to
generate and store them. Never share a password, including with IT staff — IT will never ask for it.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN and all business applications. Use the
authenticator app; SMS codes are only allowed as a temporary backup.

## Phishing and suspicious emails

Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails. Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing"
button or by forwarding them to security@lumen-analytics.example. If you think you clicked a malicious link,
raise a P1 ticket immediately.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone within 1 hour by raising a P1 ticket, so IT can lock and wipe the device
remotely. Also report the theft to the police and include the crime reference number in the ticket.

## Removable media and software

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Only install software from the company software portal;
anything else must be requested through a service desk ticket and approved by IT security.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Confidential and Restricted data must never be
uploaded to personal cloud storage or to public AI tools.
