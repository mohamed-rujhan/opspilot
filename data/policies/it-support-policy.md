# IT Support and Service Desk Policy

How employees of Lumen Analytics Ltd (a fictional company) get help from IT.

## Raising a ticket

All IT requests and incidents go through the service desk as tickets. Include a clear title, what you expected,
what happened, and any error messages. One ticket per issue.

## Ticket priorities and response times

- **P1 – Critical:** a security incident, or many people cannot work. First response within 1 hour, 24/7.
- **P2 – High:** one person cannot work, or an important deadline is at risk. First response within 4 business hours.
- **P3 – Normal:** something is broken but there is a workaround. First response within 1 business day.
- **P4 – Low:** questions, small requests and improvements. First response within 3 business days.

## Hardware refresh

Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or earlier if they fail and cannot be repaired. Monitors and accessories can
be requested with a P4 ticket.

## Software requests

Software that is not in the company software portal must be requested with a ticket explaining the business need.
IT security reviews new software within 5 business days.

## Password resets and account lockouts

Use the self-service reset page first. If you are still locked out, raise a P2 ticket from a colleague's account
or call the service desk.

## Service desk hours

The service desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UK time. P1 incidents are handled 24/7 by the
on-call engineer.
