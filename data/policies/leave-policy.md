# Leave Policy

Lumen Analytics Ltd is a fictional company used for this demo. This policy applies to all permanent employees.

## Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, plus public holidays. Part-time
employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours. New starters accrue leave from their
first day of employment.

## Booking annual leave

Annual leave must be requested through the service desk and approved by your line manager before it is taken.
Requests for more than 3 consecutive working days need at least 2 weeks' notice. During the December freeze
(15–31 December) no more than half of a team may be on leave at the same time.

## Carrying over leave

Up to 5 unused days of annual leave may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by
31 March, after which they are lost. Leave cannot be exchanged for pay except when employment ends.

## Sick leave

If you are unwell, tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. You may self-certify for up
to 7 calendar days. For absences longer than 7 calendar days a doctor's note (fit note) is required. Employees
receive full pay for up to 10 days of sick leave in any rolling 12-month period.

## Unpaid leave

Employees may request up to 10 days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons. Unpaid leave always requires
line manager approval and is not guaranteed.

## Parental leave

Birth parents are entitled to 26 weeks of maternity or adoption leave at full pay for the first 16 weeks.
Partners are entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 12 months of the birth or
adoption.
