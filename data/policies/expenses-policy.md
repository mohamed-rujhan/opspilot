# Travel and Expenses Policy

How employees of Lumen Analytics Ltd (a fictional company) claim business expenses.

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense through the expenses system. Claims are approved by your line
manager and paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every expense over £25. Card payment slips alone are not accepted as receipts.

## Meals and subsistence

When travelling on business, meals can be claimed up to £40 per day in the UK and £60 per day abroad. Alcohol is
not reimbursed unless it is part of an approved client event.

## Travel

Book rail and air travel through the company travel portal. Standard class rail and economy flights are the default;
premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 6 hours. Taxis are allowed when public transport is not
practical or after 21:00.

## Mileage

Business mileage in a personal car is reimbursed at 45p per mile. Commuting between home and your usual office is
not business mileage.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are limited to £150 per night in London and £110 per night elsewhere in the UK, including breakfast.
